Subscription and Billing Policy

Last updated: 25 August 2026

1. Definitions

Platform: the "Dashboard" web app that provides educational and scientific experiment services for students, teachers, and schools.

User: any natural or legal person who creates an account as a student, teacher, or school.

Subscription: the digital contract that connects the user to the Platform and grants paid access to services.

Trial Period: a limited time during which a new user may use the services for free.

Plan/Package: a defined set of services, limits, and pricing for a subscription.

Invoice: a digital record of payment or payment due between the user and the Platform.

Admin: the owner or authorized manager of the Platform.

Device Fingerprint: a technical identifier derived from IP address and browser data used to prevent repeated trial abuse.

2. Plans and Pricing

The Platform offers plans for students, teachers, and schools. Prices are displayed in EUR or the currency shown at checkout. Prices are reviewed periodically, and users are notified of any change before the next renewal.

3. Trial Period

  • Student: 3 calendar days.
  • Teacher: 7 calendar days.
  • School: 7 calendar days.

The trial starts at account creation. Each device is allowed only one trial. At the end of the trial, paid access stops until a paid plan is chosen.

4. Payments and Invoices

Accepted payment methods are shown at checkout. The Platform issues a digital invoice for each successful payment. The admin can view and update invoice statuses in the admin dashboard.

5. Auto-Renewal and Cancellation

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically using the same payment method. The Platform will send a reminder notification by email or dashboard at least 7 days before the automatic renewal of yearly subscriptions, giving the user the opportunity to review or cancel before the charge.

Users must cancel at least 24 hours before the next renewal. Cancellation keeps access active until the end of the paid period.

6. Refund Policy

Users may request a full refund within 14 days of purchase if the services have not been used in practice. Refund requests are processed within 7 to 14 business days.

7. User Rights and Obligations

Users have the right to access paid services, receive invoices, and manage their subscription. Users must provide accurate information, avoid fraud, not share login data, and respect plan limits.

8. Admin/Platform Rights

The Admin may suspend or terminate accounts for violations, update prices with prior notice, and monitor usage for security and compliance.

9. Anti-Fraud

The Platform records IP address and device fingerprint to prevent repeated trial abuse and suspicious behavior. Records are kept for up to 12 months.

10. Privacy and Data

Payment data is processed through a secure external provider and is not fully stored on Platform servers. User data is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

11. Account Suspension and Termination

Accounts may be suspended for late payment or security issues. Accounts may be terminated for repeated fraud, illegal use, or abusive content.

12. Policy Changes

The Platform may update this policy at any time. Users will be notified of material changes at least 14 days before they take effect. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.

13. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute will first be addressed through support. If unresolved, the matter may be referred to the competent authorities under applicable law.

14. Contact

Email: support@feras.app

15. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

The Platform commits to making reasonable efforts to maintain service continuity. In the event of an unplanned service outage lasting more than 48 continuous hours, the user will be granted a free subscription extension equal to the actual duration of the outage, provided a support request is submitted within 7 days of the outage ending.
